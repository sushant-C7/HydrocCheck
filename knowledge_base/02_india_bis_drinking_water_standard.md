# Drinking-water specifications in India

## Relevant Indian standard
The Bureau of Indian Standards catalogue identifies IS 10500:2012, Drinking Water — Specification (Second Revision). The official BIS catalogue and licensed/current standard text are the places to verify the current publication status, requirements, amendments and exact specification values.

## Compliance questions
For a question about compliance in India, prefer the applicable current BIS standard and relevant instructions from competent Indian authorities. Do not substitute a WHO guideline, a United States EPA rule, a model's training-data range, or a probability from HydroCheck for an official compliance determination.

## Limits of the HydroCheck input set
HydroCheck's bundled machine-learning dataset uses nine numeric features: pH, hardness, total dissolved solids (Solids), chloramines, sulfate, conductivity, organic carbon, trihalomethanes and turbidity. This feature list is not a complete drinking-water compliance test. In particular, a prediction from these fields does not establish that microbiological, radiological or other required parameters have been tested.

## Safe response behaviour
When asked whether a sample meets Indian drinking-water standards, state that this app cannot certify compliance. Explain that the current BIS text must be checked against valid laboratory results for all applicable parameters. Avoid quoting numeric limits unless they have been retrieved from a current, authoritative source that explicitly applies to the requested jurisdiction.
