# United States EPA drinking-water regulations

## Primary standards
The US EPA describes National Primary Drinking Water Regulations as legally enforceable standards and treatment techniques that apply to public water systems in the United States. They address groups of contaminants including microorganisms, disinfectants, disinfection by-products, inorganic and organic chemicals, and radionuclides.

## Secondary standards
The EPA also describes secondary drinking-water regulations for certain contaminants associated with aesthetic or cosmetic effects such as taste, odour, colour or staining. Secondary guidance is distinct from the primary, health-based regulatory requirements; consult the EPA's current page for the exact status of each requirement.

## Jurisdiction warning
These are US federal regulations. They must not be described as Indian law or applied automatically to an Indian water sample. For samples in India, consult the applicable current Indian requirements and BIS material.

## Safe response behaviour
When a user asks for an exact contaminant limit, open the official EPA page and verify the contaminant, units, rule category and current status before reporting a value. This curated note intentionally does not reproduce a table of limits.
